Help with access and app issues

Cannot sign in?

Your NoDrafts account is managed by your hotel or organization. Ask your hotel manager to confirm your account, assigned hotel, and permissions.

App not loading?

  • Confirm your phone has an internet connection.
  • Close and reopen the app.
  • Install the latest available version.
  • If the problem continues, include your hotel code, the screen affected, and a screenshot in your support email. Do not send your password.

Privacy or account request?

Contact your hotel administrator or email us to request correction, access revocation, or deletion of information associated with your managed employee account.

We aim to respond to support requests within two business days.

NoDrafts Housekeeping

Privacy Policy

Effective September 30, 2026

NoDrafts Housekeeping is a workforce application used by authorized hotel employees and administrators. This policy explains the information processed when you use the app.

Information we process

  • Account information supplied by your employer, such as your name, work email, user ID, role, organization, and assigned hotel.
  • Operational activity, including room assignments, checklist updates, cleaning status and timing, schedules, shift-swap requests, and incident reports.
  • Messages and other content you choose to send through workplace chat.
  • Photos you choose to attach to an incident report. Camera and photo-library access are requested only when you use that feature.
  • Technical records needed for security, reliability, and troubleshooting, such as request times and error logs.

How we use information

We use this information to authenticate users, provide hotel operations features, coordinate work, document incidents, protect the service, troubleshoot problems, and meet contractual or legal obligations.

Sharing and service providers

Information is visible to authorized users in your organization based on their role. We may use cloud hosting, storage, and communications providers to operate the service. These providers process information only to deliver services to NoDrafts and must protect it appropriately. We do not sell personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

Storage and retention

The app stores your language preference on your device. Business records are retained only as long as needed for hotel operations, security, contractual obligations, and applicable law. Retention can also be controlled by the organization that manages your account.

Your choices

You may decline camera or photo-library access and continue using features that do not require photos. Because employee accounts are created and managed by an organization, contact your hotel administrator or NoDrafts support to correct information, revoke access, or request deletion. Some operational records may be retained when required by law or legitimate business obligations.

Security and children

We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards to protect information. The app is intended for authorized workforce use and is not directed to children.

Policy updates

We may update this policy as the service changes. The effective date above identifies the latest version.

Privacy contact

Questions or data requests can be sent to feedback@nodrafts.com.
